# WIC Bid Practical Manual — Beginner Ready — 2026-10-26

## Zero rule
The exact current notice and attachments are the authority.
Past formulas, past KISTI practices and old screenshots never override the current notice.

## 1. Standing readiness
Before selecting a bid:
- confirm current G2B supplier registration/status
- confirm required business/industry/product qualification
- confirm current personal authentication method
- confirm current SME/small-business certificate when required
- keep current company documents in one controlled folder
- never submit expired archive copies without checking validity

## 2. Find notices
For KISTI, search current official procurement notices first through NaraJangter/G2B using:
한국과학기술정보연구원

Use KISTI website notices as a supplementary source for briefings/additional notices.
Do not use the dead legacy ebid.kisti.re.kr host as the primary 2026 search path.

For WIC keywords, monitor:
- 해외보고서
- 시장조사
- 시장보고서
- 데이터베이스
- 전자자료
- 도서
- 해외자료
plus known research institutions.

## 3. First 5-minute notice extraction
Always extract:
1. notice number
2. notice title
3. demand/issuing institution
4. submission deadline
5. opening time
6. contract method
7. winner-selection method
8. participant eligibility
9. estimated/base/business amount
10. VAT rule
11. bid bond
12. required documents
13. attachments
14. submission route
15. joint/region/industry/SME restrictions

Unknown means MANUAL_REQUIRED, not guessed.

## 4. Participation decision
PASS only when:
- qualification requirements are satisfied
- geographic restrictions are satisfied
- required SME status is satisfied
- report/product can actually be supplied
- publisher confirms supply where needed
- deadline is achievable

FAIL when a mandatory requirement is not met.

## 5. Price calculation
Never use fixed rules such as "KISTI = 88%".
Only use notice-confirmed:
- base amount
- expected-price method
- lower-limit rate
- preliminary-price range
- VAT
- bond
- evaluation formula

Historical KISTI evidence proves that base*88% can differ from the true threshold because the final expected price may be derived from multiple preliminary prices.

### WIC dual-axis calculation
Public procurement axis:
base/expected amount → lower limit/evaluation → bid amount

Supply-cost axis:
publisher list price → FX → discount/commission → payment costs → VAT → actual cost → minimum margin

Do not participate if the feasible procurement price is below safe supply cost.

## 6. Documents
Use the exact notice.
Typical items may include:
- bid/price submission
- qualification evidence
- SME certificate
- business documents
- performance evidence
- proposal
- specification/supply confirmation
- integrity/security pledges
- other notice-specific documents

Never add an old KISTI form merely because it was used historically.

## 7. Final pre-submit gate
Check:
- correct notice number/title
- no correction/re-bid notice supersedes it
- deadline/time
- KRW/unit
- VAT
- total vs unit price
- recalculated amount
- required files present and openable
- official final-submit screen matches intended notice

## 8. Submission
Official-site final actions:
1. login
2. open exact notice
3. enter price
4. attach required files
5. review system validation
6. final submit
7. confirm acceptance
8. save receipt/submission record

Credentials/authentication/final irreversible submit remain user-controlled.

## 9. After submission
- save submitted amount
- record opening time
- monitor correction/re-bid
- record rank and winning amount
- preserve evidence
- feed result back to institutional history

## 10. If awarded
- review award notice
- contract by stated deadline
- satisfy guarantee requirements
- order from publisher
- deliver in required license/format
- request inspection
- complete inspection
- invoice/payment request
- confirm payment
- preserve entire case record

## 11. Beginner mistakes to block
1. reuse old 88%
2. confuse base vs expected price
3. confuse VAT included/excluded
4. confuse total vs unit
5. miss correction notice
6. miss qualification-registration cutoff
7. use expired SME certificate
8. ignore attachments
9. misunderstand bond exemption
10. fail to save receipt
11. ignore publisher cost/FX/discount
12. use legacy institution portal as if current
